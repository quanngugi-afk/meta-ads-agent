ℹ️ How Binary Arbitrage Works

On Polymarket, each binary market has YES and NO tokens. One always resolves to $1.00 and the other to $0.


The opportunity: If ask(YES) + ask(NO) < $1.00, you can buy both tokens for less than $1 and always collect exactly $1 at resolution — guaranteed profit.


Example: YES ask = $0.44, NO ask = $0.52. Total cost = $0.96. Guaranteed payout = $1.00. Gross profit = $0.04 (4%) minus ~2% fees = ~2% net.


Risk: You must hold until market resolution. Liquidity may be thin. Always check the market URL before trading.
