📖 How Polymarket Arbitrage Works
A complete guide to risk-free arbitrage on prediction markets
🎯 What Is Binary Arbitrage?

Every Polymarket binary market has two tokens: YES and NO. Exactly one resolves to $1.00 at expiry — the other to $0. In a perfectly efficient market, YES price + NO price = $1.00 exactly.

In practice, thin liquidity, wide spreads, and market-maker pricing can cause the sum to temporarily fall below $1.00 — creating a guaranteed profit window.

💡 Example

Market: "Will BTC exceed $120k by Dec 2025?"

YES ask: $0.44  |  NO ask: $0.52  |  Total cost: $0.96

Buy both → guaranteed $1.00 payout → gross profit: $0.04 (4.2%)

After ~2% Polymarket fee: ~2.2% net profit

📋 How to Execute the Trade
1
Find an opportunity — ArbiScan scans markets where YES ask + NO ask < $0.98 (accounting for fees). Click any card to see the full profit breakdown.
2
Open Polymarket — Click "View Market" to go directly to the market. You need a Polymarket account funded with USDC on Polygon.
3
Buy YES tokens — Place a buy at the ask price shown. Check order book depth matches the "Max Shares" figure ArbiScan shows.
4
Buy NO tokens immediately — Prices change; execute both legs as fast as possible. The total cost of both must still be < $1.00.
5
Hold to resolution — One token pays $1.00, the other $0. Your profit is the gap between your total cost and $1.00.
⚠️ Risks to Know

Execution risk — Prices update between scan and trade. ArbiScan is not a real-time trading engine. Always verify prices on Polymarket before executing.

Liquidity risk — "Max Shares" is based on visible ask depth. Large orders may move the price. Start with small sizes to test.

Resolution risk — Markets can be disputed or voided. Read market rules before trading.

Fee risk — ArbiScan uses ~2% as the fee estimate. Confirm current rates on Polymarket — they vary by market.

❓ FAQ
Why do these opportunities exist?
Thin liquidity and wide market-maker spreads on low-volume markets cause YES + NO prices to temporarily deviate. High-volume markets are usually efficient; smaller markets are where arb appears.
How often does ArbiScan find HIGH opportunities?
HIGH (>3% net) are uncommon. MEDIUM (1–3%) appear more regularly. LOW (<1%) are frequent but may not be worthwhile after slippage and gas costs.
What is multi-outcome arbitrage?
Markets with 3+ outcomes (e.g. "Who wins the election?") should have all outcome prices sum to $1.00. If the sum of midpoint prices is below $1.00, buying all outcomes guarantees profit.
Is this legal?
Arbitrage is a standard market-efficiency mechanism and is legal. Verify Polymarket's terms and your local regulations before trading real funds.
How does the WhatsApp alert work?
The scanner runs automatically every 15 minutes. When HIGH opportunities are detected, an immediate WhatsApp alert is sent via Twilio. Reply "poly" or "arb" to trigger an on-demand scan.
